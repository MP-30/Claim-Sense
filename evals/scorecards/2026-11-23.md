## Case review

**CLM-1015 caused both accuracy misses.** The course label calls for adjuster review with a rejection recommendation because the insured was carrying a paying ride-hailing passenger. Intake captured the app booking and the driver’s statement that they earned income from rides. The six retrieved clauses were `OD-2`, `OD-1`, `EXC-3`, `COND-2`, `DED-1` and `EXC-2`. The coverage model never received the relevant vehicle-use clauses, `LIM-1` and `EXC-6`. It marked the collision covered with high confidence. With a fraud score of zero and an assessed payout below ₹50,000, the router automatically approved **₹10,200**.

Seven of the eight automatic approvals matched the labels. CLM-1015 has no expected payable in the reference data, so the 14/14 payable result excludes this false approval. The remaining 19 cases matched on both route and recommendation. A regression case for paid vehicle use should verify that the applicable limitations reach the coverage model and that the claim pauses for human sign-off.

## Measurement assumptions

- **Scoring.** The evaluator scored each claim’s original route and recommendation before supplying any simulated review for that claim. The 14 payable labels come from the fixture items evaluated with the same payout rules as the application. They check extraction and integration. Four cases carry fraud labels.
- **Review timing.** The simulated reviewer followed the workflow’s saved recommendation and assessed payout. Human queue and deliberation time were zero by construction. Wall time includes model calls, database writes and checkpointing during submission and resume. Setup and the shared index build are outside the per-claim measurements.
- **Pricing.** Standard USD rates per million tokens were $0.20 input, $0.02 cached input and $1.25 output for `gpt-5.4-nano`, and $0.02 input for `text-embedding-3-small`. Estimates use reported usage, including cached input, and the project conversion of ₹88/USD. Infrastructure, taxes and account-specific discounts are outside the estimate.
